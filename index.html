Privacy Policy

BMI Calculator

Simple calculations. Your privacy matters.

Effective date: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the BMI Calculator mobile application, developed by Shueib Abdi Mohamed (“we,” “our,” or “the developer”), handles information when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

BMI Calculator does not require you to create an account and does not collect, transmit, or store your personal information on our servers. Information you enter, such as height, weight, age, and gender, is used only to calculate your Body Mass Index (BMI) within the app.

2. How Your Information Is Used

The values you enter are used to calculate and display your BMI result and its corresponding BMI category. The app is designed to perform these calculations locally on your device. We do not use these values to identify you or build a personal profile.

3. Saving and Sharing Results

If you choose to save a result image to your device's gallery or share it using your device's sharing options, that action is initiated by you. The saved image or shared content may contain details displayed on the result screen. Please review the information before sharing it. We do not receive a copy of the image through the app.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your entered BMI information with the developer or third parties. When you choose to use an external app to share a result, that app handles the information according to its own privacy policy and practices.

5. Data Retention and Security

BMI Calculator does not maintain a developer-operated database of your calculations. Your device may retain an image if you explicitly save a result to your gallery. Any image saved or shared by you is managed through your device and the apps you choose to use.

6. Children's Privacy

BMI Calculator is not designed to collect personal information from children. Since the app does not collect or transmit personal information to our servers, we do not knowingly collect children's personal information.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's features or applicable requirements change. Any updated version will be published at this page with a revised effective date.
